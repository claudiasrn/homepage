About me

I'm a computer science student in Frankfurt who builds things end to end — Node and Express over PostgreSQL on the server, React and plain JavaScript in the browser. Lately that's meant a file storage service with its own authentication and expiring share links, and an inventory system built on hand-written SQL. I like the part of development where an idea turns into something people can actually use. Outside of code, I'm usually out hiking with my dog or repotting one of my plants.
